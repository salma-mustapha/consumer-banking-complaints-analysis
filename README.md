Consumer Banking Complaint Analysis

Consumer complaints provide insight into recurring problems consumers experience with financial products and services, making complaint patterns and resolution outcomes important indicators of consumer and operational concerns. This project analyzes 55,271 consumer complaints submitted to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) from May through July 2026 to evaluate complaint volume, banking products, complaint issues, company response outcomes, timely responses, and monetary relief.

Using SQL and Power BI, the analysis examines which financial products and issues generate the greatest complaint volume, which companies receive the most complaints, how companies respond to consumers, and how resolution outcomes vary across products. The analysis found that credit card and checking or savings account complaints accounted for approximately 76% of all complaints, while 99.07% of complaints received a timely company response. Credit card complaints also had the highest monetary relief rate at 12.63%, compared with 1.26% for mortgage complaints, highlighting differences in resolution outcomes across financial products.

The project identifies account management, purchase disputes, deposits and withdrawals, and unauthorized transactions as important areas of consumer concern and highlights major financial institutions with high complaint volumes. The analysis recommends prioritizing recurring complaint categories, strengthening dispute and transaction-resolution processes, and evaluating response timeliness alongside resolution outcomes and monetary relief to identify opportunities for improved complaint resolution.
